How to explore the patient-side Medi Route demonstration.

How to use the demo

1
Report an emergencyStart the flow, confirm a detected or manually entered pickup location, then provide a short category and review the request.
2
Review assignmentA compounder using the companion portal accepts the request and shares the ambulance device GPS location.
3
Follow the journeyOpen tracking to view a simulated route, ETA and destination. The compounder portal controls journey status and shares the current route marker.
4
Review your informationUpdate basic profile details and inspect the intake preview. No hospital receives these details.

What the labels mean

Simulated Generated by the local frontend demo, not live dispatch or GPS.

Demo data Fictional sample values for a college project presentation.

Action required A step needs your attention before the workflow can continue.

Not an emergency service
Medi Route here is an academic prototype. It cannot dispatch ambulances, notify hospitals, diagnose conditions, or control traffic signals. For a real emergency, contact local emergency services.

Demo controls

Reset returns the application to its starting state and clears the active in-memory demo journey.
